# Observations

## IoT Waste Management System
- Waste level monitoring using ultrasonic sensor was consistent and responsive across different fill conditions  
- System successfully detected threshold levels (low, medium, full, overfull)  
- Moisture sensor accurately differentiated wet and dry waste  
- GSM communication enabled reliable data transmission to cloud, with minor delay under low signal conditions  
- Dustbin overflow condition was detected correctly, and alert was triggered  

---

## Battery Management System (BMS)
- Voltage, current, and temperature parameters were monitored accurately in real time  
- System operated stably within defined limits under normal conditions  
- Protection logic for abnormal conditions (overvoltage, overcurrent) functioned as expected  
- Sensor readings showed minor fluctuations initially, improved after calibration  

---

## General Observations
- System performance improved after sensor calibration and threshold tuning  
- Real-time monitoring reduced need for manual checking  
- Communication latency observed in GSM under weak network conditions  
- Overall system demonstrated reliable operation and consistent performance
